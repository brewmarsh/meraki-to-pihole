💡 What: Added a "Skip to main content" link at the beginning of the `<body>` that targets a designated `<main>` element wrapping the application content.

🎯 Why: Users relying on keyboard navigation (like hitting the 'Tab' key) or screen readers previously had to navigate through the entire navigation bar and welcome modal elements before they could reach the primary interface. This skip link provides a fast, standard way to bypass repetitive blocks.

📸 Before/After: The link uses Bootstrap's `visually-hidden-focusable` class. It remains completely invisible to sighted mouse users and only appears visually when focused by keyboard navigation.

♿ Accessibility:
- Provides an immediate way for keyboard-only users to jump to the core content.
- Replaced the structural `<div class="container">` wrapping the content with a semantic `<main id="main-content">` tag, which improves screen reader comprehension.
- The `<main>` container is configured with `tabindex="-1"` and `outline: none` to cleanly receive the programmatic focus jump from the skip link without displaying an intrusive focus ring around the entire body.
