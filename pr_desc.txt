💡 What: Added 'Enter' key submission support for the standalone sync interval input field.
🎯 Why: Standalone inputs outside of `<form>` tags do not automatically submit on 'Enter'. This enhancement ensures keyboard users can seamlessly update the interval without needing to tab to or click the "Update" button, improving usability.
📸 Before/After: Visuals verified via Playwright, showing seamless keyboard submission.
♿ Accessibility: Ensures that keyboard-only users have standard, expected interaction paradigms when interacting with standalone settings inputs.
