💡 What: Added a "Skip to main content" link to the application's HTML templates.
🎯 Why: Keyboard-only users and screen reader users can find it tedious to tab through repetitive global navigation elements (like the welcome banner and navbar) to reach the main interactive content of the page.
📸 Before/After: N/A - The link is visually hidden until it receives keyboard focus.
♿ Accessibility: Improves WCAG compliance (Guideline 2.4.1 Bypass Blocks) by allowing keyboard and assistive technology users to directly access the `<main>` content container. The `<main>` container was also given a `tabindex="-1"` and `outline: none;` to allow it to receive programmatic focus cleanly.
